What’s being encoded?

A single-qubit angle encoding maps a classical scalar θ to |ψ⟩ = cos(θ/2)|0⟩ + sin(θ/2)|1⟩. The chart below shows the classical cos(θ)/sin(θ) for intuition; notebooks compute the actual statevectors and measurements.
