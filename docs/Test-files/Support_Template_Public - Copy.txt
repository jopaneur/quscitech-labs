QuSciTech-Labs — KDP Access Information
───────────────────────────────────────────────

Thank you for your interest in *Quantum AI Systems: Theory, Architecture, and Applications*
and its companion laboratory series — **QuSciTech-Labs**.

───────────────────────────────────────────────
How to Request Full Edition Access
───────────────────────────────────────────────

Readers who have purchased the book via **Amazon KDP** are eligible to access the complete
**QuSciTech-Labs Full Edition**, which includes:

• Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced Labs (code-enabled + no-code)
• All figures, helper scripts, and version-tracked updates
• DOI-linked archival for academic citation

To request access, please complete the **Access Request Form** at:
→ https://forms.gle/your_form_id_here

Include the following:
- Your full name
- Email address (**GitHub username preferred**)
- Amazon KDP order ID or proof of purchase
- Confirmation that you agree to the license and redistribution policy

───────────────────────────────────────────────
After Submitting Your Form
───────────────────────────────────────────────
Once your information is verified, you will receive a GitHub invitation
granting access to the **private QuSciTech-Labs Full Edition repository**.

You’ll be notified via the email address or GitHub account you provided.
