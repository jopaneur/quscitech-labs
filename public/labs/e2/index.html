E.2 — Intermediate Labs

These labs support the Intermediate track (E.2) described in Appendix E of Quantum AI Systems: Theory, Architecture, and Applications. They extend the Beginner labs into quantum communication, information metrics, and variational control.

E.2.1 — Quantum Kernel: SVM vs Logistic Regression

Compare a classical logistic regression classifier with a quantum kernel SVM, highlighting situations where quantum feature spaces offer expressive advantages.
