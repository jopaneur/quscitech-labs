Quantum AI Systems (QAIS): Architectures for Quantum AI Systems

This volume develops the representational and reasoning layers of Quantum Artificial Intelligence Systems (QAIS), including quantum encoding, semantic structure, probabilistic inference, representational architectures, and propagation-aware computational reasoning.

The materials associated with this volume support representational analysis, inferential modeling, semantic encoding workflows, reasoning-system experimentation, and hybrid quantum–classical computational exploration within adaptive QAIS environments.
