Privacy

Overview

We minimize data collection. This site has no on-site payments and no user accounts. Reviewer coordination and purchases occur on external platforms subject to their policies.

Information we collect

  • Contact form submissions: Name, email, and message content are relayed by email to the author/publisher for follow-up.
  • Server logs: Standard HTTP logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp, requested URL) retained briefly for security and reliability.
  • No tracking pixels: We do not embed third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use information

  • Respond to inquiries and coordinate reviewer access where applicable.
  • Protect site integrity, detect abuse, and troubleshoot availability issues.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Messages may be shared with trusted collaborators solely to address your request (e.g., editorial or technical support).

Retention

Contact emails are retained in normal business archives. Server logs are kept for a limited period necessary for security operations.

Security

We use industry-standard controls appropriate to a static informational site. Sensitive workflows (like purchases) occur off-site with their own security and privacy terms.

Your choices

  • Use alternate channels listed on the Contact page instead of the site form.

Changes

We may update this notice to reflect site or policy changes. Material updates will be reflected here with a revised effective date.
