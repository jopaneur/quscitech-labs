QuSciTech Labs — Track Overview (E.1 / E.2 / E.3)

This portal provides access to the E.1 (Beginner), E.2 (Intermediate), and E.3 (Advanced) tracks that accompany Quantum AI Systems: Theory, Architecture, and Applications. Each track links to a track-level landing page with Colab badges, GitHub links, and DOI-aligned guidance.

Track E.1 · Beginner

E.1 — Foundational Quantum Concepts

Focuses on single-qubit superposition, entanglement, angle encoding, circuit depth vs. noise, and the quantum teleportation protocol. Ideal for readers encountering quantum states, measurement, and circuits for the first time.

Includes 5 no-code labs with guided narratives, figures, and pre-wired Qiskit code for Google Colab and IBM Quantum backends.

Open E.1 Track →
Track E.2 · Intermediate

E.2 — Communication, Metrics & Variational Control

Extends E.1 concepts into BB84 quantum key distribution, tamper-evident entanglement checks, quantum kernel methods, expectation scans, fidelity metrics, and VQE toy minimization.

Includes 6 labs designed for readers who are comfortable with basic quantum circuits and want to explore QAIS-relevant metrics and control.

Open E.2 Track →
Track E.3 · Advanced

E.3 — Full QAIS Pipelines & Case Study Stress Tests

Integrates Bloch-sphere control, Bell tests, density-matrix encodings, hybrid quantum–classical pipelines, Grover search robustness, and the QALIS vs. CRQC–LLM resilience case study.

Includes 7 labs geared toward advanced readers, instructors, and reviewers examining full QAIS behavior under noise, interference, and architectural stress.
