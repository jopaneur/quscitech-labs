## 🧭 Overview

This index lists all public-access **Beginner Labs (E.1)** and preview **Intermediate (E.2)** and **Advanced (E.3)** stubs.  
Each lab aligns with Appendix E of *Quantum AI Systems: Theory, Architecture, and Applications*,  
bridging quantum information concepts with hands-on visualization in Google Colab.

Full editions (coded + extended no-code labs) are available through  
**Amazon KDP** and the **private QuSciTech-Labs Full Edition** repository.
