This volume develops the communication, sensing, governance, resilience, and deployment layers of Quantum Artificial Intelligence Systems (QAIS), including distributed quantum communication, sensing infrastructures, interoperability frameworks, operational governance, verification architectures, and large-scale intelligent-system deployment.

The materials associated with this volume support systems integration, communication analysis, deployment modeling, resilience engineering, governance studies, and operational experimentation across distributed QAIS ecosystems.
