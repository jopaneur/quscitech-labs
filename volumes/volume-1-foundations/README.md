Volume I — Foundations of Quantum AI Systems

Companion materials and public resources supporting:

Quantum AI Systems (QAIS): Architectures for Quantum AI Systems

This volume develops the physical, mathematical, and operational foundations underlying scalable Quantum Artificial Intelligence Systems (QAIS). Topics include quantum states, qubits, superposition, entanglement, quantum gates, circuits, measurement systems, decoherence, noise-aware architectures, and foundational hybrid quantum–classical computational frameworks.

The materials associated with this volume support foundational study, operational analysis, systems-oriented learning, and introductory laboratory exploration within quantum-enabled intelligent systems.
