E.3 — Advanced Labs

These labs support the Advanced track (E.3) in Appendix E. They integrate full QAIS workflows: Bloch-sphere control, Bell tests, density-matrix encodings, hybrid quantum–classical pipelines, Grover search, and the QALIS vs. CRQC–LLM resilience case study.
